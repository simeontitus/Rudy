QR code maker

One QR per machine page. SVG is best for Illustrator; PNG for quick tests.

Before printing a batch: print one sticker at the real size, stick it on a machine, and scan it with 2–3 different phones in sunlight. Keep the QR at least 3–4 cm wide, dark on a light background, with a white border (quiet zone) around it. Never put the QR on top of a photo or pattern.
